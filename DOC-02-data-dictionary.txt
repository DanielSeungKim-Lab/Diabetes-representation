Data dictionary

Blank cells mean not applicable or not reported unless stated otherwise.

DATA-01-trials.csv
One row per trial in either analytic cohort (369 trials).
  nct_id                ClinicalTrials.gov identifier
  in_race_cohort        1 if in the race cohort (355 trials), else 0
  in_ethnicity_cohort   1 if in the ethnicity cohort (223 trials), else 0
  race_order            Bootstrap row order within the race cohort (1 to 355)
  ethnicity_order       Bootstrap row order within the ethnicity cohort (1 to 223)
  enrollment            Registry-reported enrollment
  lead_sponsor_class    Registry lead sponsor class: INDUSTRY, OTHER, FED, NETWORK, INDIV
  site_countries        Countries with registered sites, separated by semicolons;
                        empty if no locations were posted
  n_white, n_black, n_asian, n_aian, n_nhopi, n_other_multiple
                        Participants in each harmonized race category: White; Black or
                        African American; Asian; American Indian or Alaska Native; Native
                        Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander; other or multiple race
  n_unknown_race        Participants with unknown or unreported race
  race_scheme           Source of race counts: OMB race (the registry's dedicated race
                        measure) or Free text (a customized race or race-or-ethnicity table)
  n_hispanic, n_not_hispanic, n_unknown_ethnicity
                        Participants by Hispanic or Latinx ethnicity

DATA-02-denominators.csv
US diabetes case shares used as benchmarks.
  stratification   race, ethnicity, or race_ethnicity (the five NHANES survey groups)
  scenario         primary; definition_hba1c_only; definition_self_report_only;
                   definition_fasting_glucose_composite; definition_type1_excluded;
                   primary__race_frame_hispanic_as_white; primary__race_frame_nonhispanic_only
  category         Racial or ethnic group
  share            Share of US adult diabetes cases (sums to 1 within stratification and scenario)
  relse_pct        Relative standard error of the share, in percent; blank for the allocated
                   six-category race shares

DATA-03-nhanes-person-level.parquet
NHANES 2017 to March 2020 prepandemic records for adults aged 20 years and older (9,232 rows).
Variables keep their NHANES names; see the CDC NHANES documentation.
  SEQN       Respondent identifier
  RIDAGEYR   Age in years
  RIDRETH3   Race and Hispanic origin
  WTMECPRP   Examination weight
  SDMVSTRA   Design stratum
  SDMVPSU    Design primary sampling unit
  LBXGH      Glycated hemoglobin (%)
  DIQ010     Ever told by a doctor you have diabetes
  DIQ050     Now taking insulin
  DIQ070     Now taking diabetic pills
CODE-02 adds DID040 (age at diagnosis) and the fasting glucose variables (LBXGLU, WTSAFPRP)
from the public P_DIQ and P_GLU files.

DATA-04-acs-adult20-frame.json
American Community Survey 2019 to 2023 five-year national counts: adults aged 20 years and
older by sex from tables B01001A to B01001I, and all-age counts from table B03002. Used by
CODE-02.

DATA-05-race-label-dictionary.csv
Every registry race label in the race cohort and its harmonized category.
  scheme                Dedicated race table or combined race-or-ethnicity table
  registry_label        Label as posted
  harmonized_category   Category assigned; labels marked "coverage only" count toward
                        reporting coverage but not toward race shares
  n_trials              Trials using the label
  participants          Participants under the label

DATA-06-trial-characteristics.csv
One row per trial (369 trials).
  nct_id              ClinicalTrials.gov identifier
  phase               Registry phase (PHASE3, PHASE4, or PHASE2;PHASE3)
  start_date          Start date (year and month)
  start_year          Start year
  minimum_age         Minimum eligible age as posted
  minimum_age_years   Minimum eligible age in years
  maximum_age         Maximum eligible age as posted
  std_ages            Registry age groups (CHILD, ADULT, OLDER_ADULT)

DATA-07-us-only-facilities.csv
One row per facility record of the 81 US-only trials (987 records).
  nct_id, facility, city, state, country
  location_status     Site recruitment status as posted (blank for all records)

DATA-08-acs-state-population.csv
American Community Survey 2019 to 2023 five-year counts, table B02001, all ages, for the 50
states and the District of Columbia.
  state, state_fips, total_pop, black_alone, asian_alone

DATA-09-screening-disposition.csv
One row per screened record with a baseline module (612 records).
  nct_id              ClinicalTrials.gov identifier
  step                Selection step at which the record left the flow (for example
                      "2 race measure"), or "6 included"
  disposition         Reason for exclusion, or inclusion in the race cohort
  phase, start_date, enrollment, lead_sponsor_class
                      As in DATA-01 and DATA-06
  in_race_cohort, in_ethnicity_cohort
                      As in DATA-01
